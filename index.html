ACE Content Uploader

ACE Content Uploader is a private tool that manages a small group of YouTube channels run by its owner and a few collaborators. It is not offered to the public.

With the access each channel owner grants, the tool:

  • uploads videos and sets their title, description, thumbnail, playlist and publishing time,
  • reads channel and video statistics (YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API) to see how the videos perform,
  • reads public comments on the channels' own videos and posts replies as the channel.

It only acts on channels whose owners signed in and agreed. How data is handled is described in the privacy policy.
