Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy covers ACE Content Uploader (the "tool"), a private tool for managing YouTube channels run by its owner and a few collaborators.

Who is responsible

Lucas Christiani, lucas.christiani97@gmail.com

What the tool accesses

After a channel owner signs in with Google and grants access, the tool uses the YouTube API Services to:

  • upload videos and edit their metadata (title, description, thumbnail, playlists, publishing time),
  • read channel and video statistics, including YouTube Analytics reports,
  • read public comments on the channel's own videos and post replies as the channel.

The tool does not access email, contacts, files or any Google data outside YouTube.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to run the channels named above: publishing videos, measuring their performance and answering comments.
  • OAuth tokens and fetched statistics are stored on the owner's own computers. They are not sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • To draft comment replies, the text of public comments is sent to an AI service (Anthropic Claude or OpenAI) that writes the reply. Nothing else is sent there.

YouTube and Google

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you also agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access and deleting data

You can revoke the tool's access at any time on the Google security settings page. To have stored data deleted, write to the address above; it is deleted within 30 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top is updated.
